Spora

A calm record book for mushroom growers. Cultures, batches, checks, harvests, orders and labels, on your phone, working offline in the grow room.

Spora is in early access and free. It is made in Oslo by a working gourmet mushroom farm.

Questions or want to try it? oslonach@gmail.com
