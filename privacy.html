Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Spora is a record-keeping app for mushroom growers. This page explains what personal data it handles, why, who helps us run it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

  • Your records live on your phone first. You choose whether they stay only there, sync to our cloud, or go to the cloud only as an encrypted backup that we cannot read.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports help us improve the app. One switch in Settings turns both off.
  • You can export everything to Excel and delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Hubert Burton, Oslo, Norway. Email: hello@getspora.app.

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, as it does in Norway through the Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven).

Where your data is kept

In Settings, Privacy & security, "Where your data is kept", each phone chooses one of three:

  • Only on this phone. Nothing is sent to us, even when you are signed in, and team features are off. The app reminds you once a week to save a backup to Files or Drive.
  • Phone and cloud. The normal sync between your phones and your team. Synced data is stored in the EU (Ireland). You can keep strains, genetics and lineage on the phone only; they are then never uploaded, and you can delete the copy already in the cloud.
  • Phone and encrypted cloud backup. Records are not synced. Once a day the phone locks a full backup, including photos and voice notes that exist only on the phone, and uploads only the locked file. The key comes from your passphrase and a printable recovery key. Our servers store only the encrypted file and cannot read it. If you lose both the passphrase and the recovery key, nobody can restore that backup, including us.

When you leave the cloud, the app offers to delete your cloud copy as well. Your account and sign-in stay until you delete the account.

Payments

Spora is free during early access, and the app takes no payments. If paid plans come later, purchases will go through Apple or Google, and we will never see your card details. We will update this page before that happens.

Who can see your data

  • You, and the people in your farm. Members see the farm's records and who made each entry, as their role allows.
  • Us, only when needed to run the service, fix a problem you report, or when the law requires it.
  • Photos are stored at long, random web addresses that cannot be guessed or listed, but anyone who has the exact address can open the image. Please don't attach photos you consider secret. Voice notes are kept private and played through short-lived links.

How long we keep it

  • Account and records: until you delete them or your account. Deleted records stay in the app's Trash for 30 days, or until you empty it.
  • Encrypted cloud backups: the newest 3, until you delete them, leave the encrypted backup option and choose to delete them, or delete your account.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: up to 24 months.
  • Backups on your phone: the last 7 days. They are removed when you delete the app.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, object to usage statistics, or ask us to pause processing. Most of this you can do yourself: Settings, Data exports everything to Excel or a full backup file, and Settings, Delete account removes your account. For anything else, email us. We answer within one month.

If your farm entered data about you as a customer or a team member, you can ask the farm, or email us and we will help.

You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

Deleting your account

See Delete your account for what is removed and how to ask if you can't open the app.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. The database on your phone is encrypted, with its key kept in the phone's secure storage. Each farm's records are kept apart by access rules in the cloud database, and you can lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and Datatilsynet as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
