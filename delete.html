Delete your account

Spora, by Hubert Burton

In the app

Open Spora and go to Settings → Delete account. Confirm with your password or email address, and it is done straight away. It can't be undone, so export a backup first if you want to keep your records (Settings → Data).

Without the app

Email hello@getspora.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Spora account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your login and profile.
  • If you are the only person in your farm: the farm and all its records, including batches, strains, recipes, stock, harvests, plans, orders, customers and history.
  • If others share the farm with you: your login and profile. The farm's records stay with the others.
  • Your photo files.
  • Usage statistics in our database linked to your account. Copies held by our analytics provider are removed within 30 days if you ask.
  • The copy of your data on that phone.

Backups you exported yourself are yours, and we can't reach them.
